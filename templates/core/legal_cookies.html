Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that the site or associated services store in your browser to remember preferences, maintain sessions, or measure use of TradeFlow Colón. Similar technologies (local storage, pixels) are handled with the same transparency criteria.

2. Essential cookies

These are necessary for basic Platform operation. Without them you could not, for example:

  • Maintain your login session securely.
  • Store your shopping cart while you browse.
  • Apply CSRF protection on forms and security preferences.

These cookies do not require prior consent to the extent they are strictly necessary.

3. Analytics cookies

These help us understand traffic and aggregated behavior (pages visited, duration, device). When enabled, we may use services such as Google Analytics or equivalent tools, always seeking configurations that limit direct identification.

4. Functional cookies

These remember your choices to improve the experience: language, store filters, dismissed banners, or interface preferences in buyer and seller panels.

5. Third-party cookies

When integrating payment services, maps, players, or external widgets, those providers may set their own cookies subject to their policies. TradeFlow does not fully control those technologies; we recommend reviewing the corresponding provider’s documentation when you complete a payment or use a linked service.

6. How to manage or disable cookies

You can configure your browser to block or delete cookies:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies.
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security.
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy.
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions.

If you disable essential cookies, some features (login, cart, checkout) may stop working correctly.
